# License & Trial

## Try Pro free for 30 days

Every Zentro install can start a **30-day Pro trial** that unlocks all Pro features. When it ends, Zentro goes back to the Free tier — nothing is lost, and Free never expires.

<Callout type="info">
  **No credit card required.** The trial is tied to one email address and one installation, so each can start it once.
</Callout>

### Start the trial

<Steps>
  ### Open the License settings
  In Zentro, open **Settings → License**. You'll also see a **Start 30-day Pro trial** button on the welcome screen and wherever a Pro feature asks for Pro.

  ### Choose "Start 30-day Pro trial"
  Enter your email and confirm. If Zentro asks you to verify the address, it sends you a confirmation link — open it, then return to the app.

  ### You're on Pro
  Pro features unlock as soon as the trial is confirmed. The License settings show your status as a Pro trial with the days remaining. There's no license key to type and no restart needed.
</Steps>

## Upgrading to Pro

Choose **Upgrade to Pro** in **Settings → License** (or in any Pro upgrade prompt). Zentro opens a secure checkout page in your browser, ready for the email you saved in **Email for Pro access**. After the payment completes, return to Zentro — the status changes to **Pro is active** (choose **Refresh** if it hasn't updated yet). For current pricing, see the [pricing page](https://zentro-ide.com/pricing).

Pro includes **priority email support**: write to [support@zentro-ide.com](mailto:support@zentro-ide.com) from the email on your license and your question is answered ahead of the general queue.

## How licensing works

Your license is tied to your **email** and this machine's **installation ID**. Zentro checks your status with the license server and caches a signed entitlement locally, so the app keeps working offline for a while (see **Offline Grace Period** in [Free vs Pro](/pro/overview)) between refreshes.

The License settings show your current status — Free plan, Pro trial (with days remaining), Pro is active, or Needs attention — along with your project limit, the masked license key, the next refresh, the plan's expiry (if any), and the offline grace expiry.

## When the trial ends

Pro features lock again and Zentro continues on the Free tier, with the Free-tier limits (for example, up to 5 projects). Your data stays on your machine, untouched. You can upgrade at any time from **Settings → License**.

## Moving to a new device

Reinstalling or switching machines? Open **Settings → License** and choose **Restore license**. When the email already has an active Pro plan, Zentro sends a confirmation link to that address first — open it, and the license activates on the new device.

## Managing your subscription

To cancel or change your plan, use the billing management link in your purchase receipt email. Cancelling reverts Zentro to the Free tier at the end of the current billing period.

## Community Edition

If you build Zentro yourself from the open-source core, you get **Zentro Community Edition**: every Free feature, no Pro code, and no contact with the license server — so the trial and upgrade options don't appear there. The official Zentro download is the build that includes Pro.
