# Free vs Pro

Zentro has two tiers: **Free** (forever) and **Pro** (subscription, $9.9 per user per month). Every install can try all of Pro free for 30 days.

<Callout type="info">
  **Try Pro free for 30 days.** No credit card required. In Zentro, open **Settings → License** and choose **Start 30-day Pro trial**. [How the trial works →](/pro/license)
</Callout>

## Core Philosophy

Safety is not a Pro gate — it's built into every tier. Free includes safety *awareness* so every developer builds the right habits from day one. Pro raises the enforcement level and removes limits.

## Offline Grace Period

| Plan | Grace period after last license check |
|---|---|
| Free | 24 hours |
| Pro | 7 days |

After the grace period, Zentro reverts to Free behavior until the license is refreshed with an internet connection.

## Pricing

Pro is $9.9 per user per month, billed monthly after your 30-day trial. Current pricing is always on the [Zentro pricing page](https://zentro-ide.com/pricing).

**Priority email support** is part of Pro: write to [support@zentro-ide.com](mailto:support@zentro-ide.com) from the email on your license and your question is answered ahead of the general queue. Free users are supported too, by email, GitHub issues, and discussions.
